01 · Foundation

Design principles

The interface should reduce decision pressure without hiding useful information.

01

Protect attention

One clear next action leads. Secondary work recedes until it is useful.

02

Explain the plan

Recommendations include timing, effort, and a human-readable reason.

03

Honor capacity

Energy, breaks, and finish lines are planning inputs, not afterthoughts.

04

Use forgiving language

Work is parked, moved, or completed. It is never framed as personal failure.
